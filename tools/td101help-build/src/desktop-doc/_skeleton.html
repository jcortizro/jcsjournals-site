How To Get Into Transition Diet 101

On a computer. Scroll down and do one picture at a time.

CLICK HERE TO ENROLL: https://live.mucusfreelife.com/td101

1.  Fill in the form

  • Type your first name, last name and email.
  • Pick your country code, then type your phone number.
  • Click the little square to agree to the terms.
  • Click the green ENROLL ME FREE button.

2.  You are enrolled

  • The page says Congratulations.
  • Scroll down to see what happens next.

3.  Go to your email

  • Two emails are on their way to you.
  • You want the one about your invitation.
  • Nothing there? Wait five minutes, then look in Spam or Promotions.

4.  Open the invitation email

  • It says "Prof. Spira has invited you to join".
  • Click it to open it.

5.  Click Accept invitation

  • The blue button in the middle of the email.
  • It opens a new page where you make your account.

6.  Make your account

  • Type your full name.
  • Make up a password and type the same one into both boxes.
  • Write that password down somewhere. You will want it later.
  • Tick the box, then click the blue Accept invitation.

7.  Create your profile

  • Type your full name in the box.
  • A photo is not needed. Leave the rest empty.
  • Scroll to the bottom and click the blue Continue button.

8.  You are inside

  • A welcome box says hello by name.
  • Click the black Get started button.

9.  Open the checklist

  • A checklist opens on the right hand side of the screen.
  • Click the line that says Start the Transition Diet 101 Course.
  • That takes you into the course.

  • 10.  The disclaimer page
  • One page to read before you begin.
  • Click the white Complete button at the bottom.
  • That takes you straight into the first lesson.

  • 11.  Start watching
  • Press play on the video.
  • When the lesson finishes, click Complete at the bottom.
  • That moves you to the next lesson. Do that every time.

  • 12.  If the checklist is not there
  • On a computer the checklist lives behind the tick icon in the top right corner.
  • Click it any time to bring the checklist back.
  • You can also click Transition Diet 101 in the list down the left side.

Stuck on any step?
